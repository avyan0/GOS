# Gods of Space — store page draft

**Short description (tagline):** Hold five lanes against 36 kinds of alien with 43 weapons, one turn at a time.

**Description:**
Aliens are marching on the last six worlds. Each turn you fire three weapons — rain meteors on the whole
field, drag a lane backwards with a gravity well, hypnotise the front alien so it fights its own side —
then watch every alien with a special ability take its turn before the whole horde advances.

- 180 levels across 6 planets, each ending harder than it starts
- 43 weapons in four rarities, each with its own trick; upgrade them with gems
- 36 aliens that heal, shield, steal gold, split apart, raise the dead or cap your damage
- 28 random events that can turn a battle — meteor showers, gravity surges, supply drops, black holes
- Hover anything in battle to see exactly what it does

Controls: mouse, or A/S/D to fire, 1–5 to pick a lane, Enter to end the turn, Space to fast-forward.
